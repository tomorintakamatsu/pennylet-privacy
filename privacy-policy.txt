PennyLet is a local-first iPhone and iPad budgeting app. Its core features work without a PennyLet
account, bank login, Plaid connection, or cloud finance sync.
Plain-language summary: PennyLet works from a protected local database. Private iCloud Backup can
keep a recovery copy so your data can return after reinstalling. Online AI is separate and optional; when
you allow and use it, PennyLet sends only the prepared text needed for that request, not your full
database, bank credentials, or receipt photo.
1. Information you choose to add
You may add transactions, amounts, dates, merchant names, notes, categories, accounts, budgets,
payday details, goals, subscriptions, watchlists, custom categories, CSV files, wallpaper profiles, and
other preferences. PennyLet uses this information to provide the features you request.
AI results and Ask PennyLet conversations may also be saved locally so you can view their history.
PennyLet does not require your name, email address, bank username, bank password, or payment-card
credentials.
2. Information stored on your device
Your budgeting records, imported transactions, review decisions, subscriptions, goals, watchlists, AI
history, and appearance profiles are stored in PennyLet's protected app storage on your device.
PennyLet does not require a PennyLet account, bank login, or connected-finance service.
You control how long local data remains. You can export transactions, clear Ask PennyLet history, or use
Reset to remove PennyLet data. Files you export are stored wherever you choose and are then
controlled by you and the selected storage provider.
3. Private iCloud Backup
Private iCloud Backup is enabled by default when iCloud is available. It keeps separate, dated saves
containing your budgeting records, preferences, watchlists, AI history, and saved wallpaper profiles. An
empty installation can restore the latest completed save after you reinstall PennyLet or install it on
another device signed into the same iCloud account. You can use core budgeting without iCloud.
The recovery copy is stored in your private Apple CloudKit database, is accessible only to your iCloud
user by default, counts against your iCloud storage quota, and is not visible to PennyLet's developer
through the developer portal. It is not sent to Cloudflare or the online AI provider.
This is recovery backup, not live multi-device sync. Restore from iCloud opens your saved history so you
can choose a date and time. Restoring replaces data on that device, not your other iCloud saves. Saves
accumulate without automatic pruning and use your iCloud storage. You can back up, restore, or turn
backup off under Settings > Data.
The separate Back up when I open PennyLet setting is on by default. It requests a save when the app
starts a new session, when iCloud and a connection are available. Switching back to an app that is
already running does not start another backup. Changes stay saved on your device until the next app
start or a manual backup. Turning this setting off keeps existing saves and manual backups available.

Deleting PennyLet from a device does not delete its iCloud saves. Reset clears this device but keeps
saved backups. After a reset, automatic backup is paused until you restore or choose Save and Resume,
which adds a new save and keeps earlier ones. To delete all indexed iCloud recovery saves, turn backup
off in Settings > Data and confirm. A small record of that choice remains, without your financial data, so a
reinstall can remember it. If deletion is interrupted, retry it to finish removing the saved files. Exported files
are not deleted by Reset.
Backups need a working iCloud account, connection, and available storage. A failed upload leaves your
local data in place, but the cloud copy may be older. Check the backup status and last successful backup
in Settings > Data before deleting the app.
Pro subscription access and permission for online AI are not restored from this backup. Apple StoreKit
checks Pro separately using the Apple Account that purchased it, and a restored installation asks again
before sending a new online AI request.
4. Optional online AI
Online AI is off until you allow it. You can turn it off again in PennyLet Settings under Legal.
When you allow and use an online AI feature, PennyLet sends a limited text request through PennyLet's
Cloudflare Worker to the external AI processor DeepSeek. Depending on the feature, that request can
contain:
- a prepared summary of saved transactions, totals, budgets, goals, subscriptions, payday details, or
other relevant PennyLet records;
- your Ask PennyLet question, relevant saved labels and names to identify what you asked about, then
app-calculated results to explain the answer;
- a watchlist name and batches of current-month merchant, category, and description text used to
identify matching entries; watchlist matching does not send transaction amounts or dates; or
- recognized receipt text used to extract merchant, amount, date, category, and line-item details.
PennyLet minimizes each request for its purpose. It does not intentionally include bank credentials, card
credentials, advertising identifiers, a PennyLet account identity, or your full local database.
These requests are not guaranteed to be anonymous. Questions, notes, merchant labels, and
recognized receipt text may contain information that identifies you. The service also authenticates
requests using an installation identifier. Please leave out personal details that are not needed for your
question.
Online AI results are generated for reference and may be incomplete or inaccurate. Review important
amounts against your saved records. PennyLet does not provide financial, investment, tax, or legal
advice.
5. Receipt scanning
Receipt scanning asks for Online AI permission before starting. Apple camera and text-recognition
frameworks read the receipt image on your device; recognized text is then sent for structured extraction.
The receipt photo is not uploaded. If the online request fails, PennyLet may offer locally recognized
details for you to review. You can always enter a transaction manually without online AI.

6. Online processors and network information
Cloudflare delivers and protects PennyLet's AI endpoint. Cloudflare may process network information
such as an IP address, request timing, routing information, and security metadata. PennyLet's Worker is
configured not to intentionally log prompts, financial summaries, receipt text, or AI responses. Limited
technical error and security records may be retained for troubleshooting, rate limiting, and abuse
prevention.
DeepSeek processes the limited text request and returns the AI response. DeepSeek's Open Platform
terms require downstream apps to provide their own privacy notice and consent. DeepSeek does not
publish a guaranteed zero-retention period for every API request. Processing may occur in the People's
Republic of China. Do not include information in questions, notes, watchlist names, or receipts that you
do not want processed by this optional service.
These providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy commitments. PennyLet
does not authorize them to use PennyLet requests for advertising or cross-app tracking. Their services
have separate security, legal-request, and retention obligations that PennyLet does not control.

7. Security and abuse prevention
PennyLet uses Apple App Attest when available to help verify genuine installations. An Apple-generated
app-install key identifier, cryptographic proof, counters, environment information, and security
timestamps may be processed by PennyLet's Cloudflare Worker. These records are used for fraud
prevention and request limiting, not advertising, and are not added to the AI prompt.
The installation identifier is persistent security data, not an advertising ID. Security records can remain on
the service after you delete the app; the current service does not automatically expire every registered
installation record. Reset clears your local budget, not its saved iCloud backup or records already
processed by external services. Contact us for a request concerning service-side data.
8. Other services
- Apple StoreKit: Apple provides localized Pro prices and handles purchases, billing, restoration, and
subscription status. PennyLet does not receive your full payment-card details.
- Apple CloudKit: If Private iCloud Backup is enabled, Apple stores PennyLet's recovery archive in
your private iCloud database.
- Apple subscription settings: PennyLet can open Apple's subscription-management page. PennyLet
cannot read every subscription in your Apple Account.
- Frankfurter: Optional exchange-rate requests send the source and destination currency codes.
PennyLet does not send transaction descriptions, balances, or bank credentials with those requests.
- Apple notifications: Payday reminders, check reminders, goal completion, and Ask PennyLet
completion alerts use iOS notification permission. Notifications do not include paycheck amounts, goal
names, or AI answers. A running AI request may have limited time to finish after you leave the app;
iOS does not guarantee continuous background work.

9. What PennyLet does not do
PennyLet does not sell or rent your data, run advertising trackers, use your data for targeted advertising,
require bank credentials, connect through Plaid, or require cloud finance sync. The current app does not
include third-party advertising or product-analytics SDKs.
10. Your choices and requests
You can decline Online AI Processing and still use PennyLet's core budgeting tools. You can later
disable Online AI Processing, clear local AI conversations, export transactions, correct saved records, or
reset the app. Turning online AI off cancels pending app requests and prevents them from sending or
retrying. Turning it back on does not resume those canceled requests. Cancellation cannot recall data
already received by an external provider or guarantee that the provider stops processing it.
PennyLet does not operate its own user-account or cloud-budget database. We generally cannot identify
or retrieve local data from your device, and the developer cannot view your private CloudKit backup in the
developer portal. For privacy questions or a request concerning data handled through PennyLet's
services, contact us using the address below. We may need limited information to verify and respond to a
request.
11. Children and families
PennyLet is a general budgeting utility and is not directed to young children. Anyone who is not old
enough to consent to online data processing where they live should use PennyLet with a parent or
guardian and leave Online AI Processing off unless that adult approves it.
12. International processing
If you use online AI, limited request data may be processed outside your country, including in the United
States and the People's Republic of China, subject to the providers' terms and applicable law.
13. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy when PennyLet's features, service providers, or legal obligations change. The
effective date at the top identifies the current version. If a change materially affects optional online
processing, PennyLet will request new consent when required.
