PennyLet Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

1. Data We Collect

PennyLet collects the following data that you provide:

- Transaction data: amounts, categories, dates, notes, and merchant names you enter

- Budget data: income, savings goals, and expense limits you set

- Receipt images: photos you scan for receipt capture

- Subscription information: detected App Store subscriptions, with your permission

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to provide the app's features:

- Display spending summaries, charts, and AI-powered insights

- Track budgets, goals, and subscriptions

- Sync data to your account for backup and multi-device access when you create an account

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on your device and synced to our secure cloud backend when you create an account. You can use the app in guest mode without creating an account. In guest mode, data stays only on your device.

4. AI Features

AI analysis features send financial summaries to our AI service for processing. We avoid sending personally identifiable information in these requests. AI features are used only to provide PennyLet's finance insights.

5. Your Rights

You can:

- Export all your data at any time from Settings > Export CSV

- Delete app data by using Settings > Export & Reset

- Request full account and data deletion by contacting us

6. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact: clearspend.app@gmail.com
